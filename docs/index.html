Orbit

Orbit (AS204921): anycast DNS, a public resolver, Shield, RPKI, Verify, Pages, Realtime, Stream, VOD, Mail, Meet and V-Gate. Free for non-commercial use.

Products

  • Public ResolverFree anycast public DNS: 177.177.83.83 resolves everything, 177.177.83.84 filters ads and malware, 177.177.83.64 adds DNS64. DoH, DoT and DoQ. No query logs.
  • RPKIHosted RPKI for networks with their own addresses: create ROAs in a console, publish to our repository, feed routers via RTR. Free: one CA, 50 ROAs.
  • VerifyA CAPTCHA alternative for web forms: invisible for most visitors, one click only when risk is high, no cookies or tracking. Free for non-commercial sites.
  • LinkA connector on the origin makes one outbound connection to the Orbit edge; Shield serves the site through it. No inbound ports, no public address.
  • PagesUpload a folder; it is served from every location we run, on your own hostname, with a certificate and Shield’s edge rules. Free for up to two projects.
  • RealtimeVoice and video rooms carried on the Orbit network. Join by link with nothing to install, or put a room inside your own page with one script tag.
  • StreamPublish a live broadcast over RTMPS from the software you already use; viewers watch over HLS, delivered from the same edge that serves every Orbit site.
  • VODUpload a video once. It is transcoded on our own nodes into a ladder of qualities and served from vod.yunzheng.space, on the same edge as every Orbit site.
  • TalkAdd chat to a product or a broadcast with one script tag. Conversations keep history; live chat keeps the last few hundred lines. Free to start.
  • MailMailboxes, groups and aliases on your own domain, a complete web mailbox for everyone in the organisation, and one console to run it. Free for personal use.
  • MeetVideo meetings in the browser: waiting room, screen sharing, captions, breakout rooms, polls and recordings, with organisation policies in one console.
  • V-GateA hosted phone system for your organisation: extensions, IVR menus, ring groups, queues, business hours and voicemail, answered in a browser or on desk phones.

Apps built on Orbit

  • YunZheng VoiceA phone in your browser: calls on your extension, voicemail, call history, forwarding and queue sign-in, plus a free number that rings all your devices.
  • YunZheng MeetFree online meetings in your browser: no download, guests join from a link. Waiting room, screen sharing, live captions, breakout rooms, polls and cloud recording.
  • YunZheng MailWeb mail on your own domain: conversations, labels, filters, undo send, snooze, search, shared mailboxes and groups. Free for personal use.

For developers

  • Orbit APIOne REST API for everything the console does, with an OpenAPI document.
  • orbit-vgate-examplesRunnable examples for the phone system API: list and import extensions, export call logs, Voice numbers.
